# Reading Notes
## SQR3
### Survey: Skim the text and identify main ideas
- This article offers a survey of the development of agentic AI technology, as well as the various applications for which such technologies may be employed. In doing so, Explore the ethical risks or challenges associated with these technologies, and they offer a framework for ethical agentic AI integration. Overall, they describe their paper as "a comprehensive introduction to Agentic AI, guiding researchers, developers, and policy makers in engaging with its transformative potential responsibly and creatively" (p. 18912).

### Query: Write questions to consider as you read
- Based on the section of the article that explores foundational concepts and definitions for agentic AI, How do the authors describe this technology in a way that is distinct from other forms of AI and machine learning? 

- What kinds of contexts do the authors suggest will be the most apt for deploying agentic AI technologies? Is their focus on private industry sectors, or do they also suggest the use of agentic AI in public contacts including government and education as well?

### Recite: Consider what you want to remember, and put that into your own words
This article read more like a glossary or atlas of Agentic AI more than anything else-- it does a nice job of surveying all of the kinds of design choices that go into the development of agentic technologies, including different methodologies or paradigms for AI training and overseeing machine learning, but its attentiveness to ethical issues ground the development and deployment of AI technologies is fairly limited.

### Relate: Identify connections or links to other readings and scholarship
I could see some connections to the Comes et al. (2025) reading, particularly because that chapter covers the authors' own design considerations in the development of an algorithm that would make planning decisions in the case of placement of healthcare facilities within refugee settlements.

## Intervention, Argument, Evidence
### Intervention: What is the intervention that the scholar is making, and where is that intervention being made? In other words, how do you place that intervention relative to existing disciplinary conversations that are taking place?
Given the institutional and industry contacts that the authors are operating within, it makes sense that the major intervention of the article is to offer a comprehensive survey of issues or variables and factories for designers, engineers, programmers, planners, and so on to take into account when developing and deploying agentic AI technologies or systems for a variety of contexts.

### Argument: What are the core argument(s) being made in the text?
- Forming conceptual framework / survey of agentic AI
- Suggests that agentic AI, due to its ability to act indepenently or self-direct its own goals and actions, require increased attention to issues of AI ethics

### Evidence: What evidence is used to support the argument(s)?
- Case studies of various industry contexts in which agentic AI technologies are being deployed (i.e. healthcare, finance, urban infrastructure management)
