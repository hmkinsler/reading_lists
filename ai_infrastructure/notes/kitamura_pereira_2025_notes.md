### Relate: Identify connections or links to other readings and scholarship
I see a lot of connections to some of the issues that are raised in Bratton's book, especially as it pertains both to the ongoing hungry search for data that is characteristic of planetary computation, as well as the way that The Stack becomes instantiated locally and globally via the City Layer. 

I likewise thought there was some interesting discussion around the issue of transparency, which has also brought up in Holt and Vonderau (2017). In particular, I found it interesting that they point out how surveillance companies such as Flock Safety, Inc. Advertise their transparency portals as it means by which consumers and the public at large can gain insight into how their platform operates in the kind of data that it's collecting. On the other hand, however, There are design choices and security vulnerabilities that are present in these ALPR cameras that are clearly not being disclosed, which seems to be a deliberate choice on the part of Flock. For example, independent researchers have found that many of the flock safety Condor cameras that have the tilt pan zoom capabilities had unsecured ports that were leaking live data feeds that could be accessed by anyone who came across the correct address for them. Although such security vulnerabilities have been reported to flock safety several months prior before going public, a disclosure practice that is common within white hat hacker communities, The company continues to deny that any such issues took place .

Of course, too, There are ample opportunities to connect a forthcoming paper such as this one to literature on surveillance capitalism, sousveillance, or control societies.
