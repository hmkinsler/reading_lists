# Reading Notes
## SQR3
### Survey: Skim the text and identify main ideas
- Though a very brief article, Suchman seeks to interrogate The discursive tendency of scholars to describe AI as a singular entity, or "thing", writing "my argument is that the thingness of AI, its status as a stable and a gentle entity, needs to be made controversial: that we need to prioritize critical engagement with the work being done by the figure of AI in specific contexts. To let the term pass is to miss the opportunity to trace its sources of power into demystify its referents" (p. 1).

- In other words, She is making the call for descriptions of AI that are always contextualized, whether for AI's role in a broader history of computational and cognitive science, Its use as both a technique and technology, or braoad signifier that is linked to flows of social, political, and economic capital.
