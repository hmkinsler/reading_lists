## Citation Information

# Reading Notes
## SQR3
### Survey: Skim the text and identify main ideas
- Kittler explores the material implications of the development of computing hardware, Serving as the logic or The substrate that enables programming and software development. He considers how word processors set upon many layers of minerals and materials that comprise computer chips and processors, the countless lines of code that dictate are machines behavior, programs that in many cases may be proprietary. Ultimately, his overall argument seeks to redirect users attention from the software interfaces that Give give a misleading sense of direct access to the digital text that one seeks to engage with , to the materiality of the hardware and software that enables the mediation of text in a digital form.
