### Survey: Skim the text and identify main ideas
- This article, or manifesto rather, Looks at a distinctly crip technoscience that is informed by a disability perspective
- They open by highlighting how crip perspectives on design, technology, and science Have been subjugated in such a way that disabled people are often not considered when it comes to technological design and innovation, with ableist ontologies and epistemology frequently being materialized in a technological world
for this reason, the authors consider how a crip perspective in conversation with feminist technoscience Enables a critical perspective on technoscience projects involving design , engineering, and political activism
- In contrast to dominant forms of knowing-making _for_ disability, we invoke crip technoscience to describe politicized practices of non-compliant  knowing-making: world-building and world-dismantling practices _by_ and _with_ disabled people and communities that respond to intersectional systems of power, privilege, and oppression by working within and around them" (pp. 4-5)
- They highlight four commitments of crip technoscience:
    - (1) "Crip technoscience centers the work of disabled people as knowers and makers" (p. 7)
    - (2) "Crip technoscience is committed to access as friction" (p. 10)
    - (3) "Crip technoscience Is committed to interdependence as political technology" (p. 12)
    - (4) "Crip Technoscience is committed to disability justice" (p. 16)

## Intervention, Argument, Evidence
### Intervention: What is the intervention that the scholar is making, and where is that intervention being made? In other words, how do you place that intervention relative to existing disciplinary conversations that are taking place?
- The major intervention that the authors seem to be making here has to do with responding to A lack of focus on distinctly Crip approaches to technoscience and STS . In particular, they argue that while STS and feminist technoscience has previously been interested in understanding politics of science and technology As a field of study as well as a practice in developing technology and furthering scientific research, that many of the actors who have engaged in this work or either able bodied, Or in other cases, the works of disable technologists and designers have been minimized and erased. Here, the authors argue that a crip technoscience argues explicitly in favor of embracing disabled pluriversality in the development of science and technology , especially in ways that further disability justice, reject ableist norms and compulsory able bodiedness, or position disabled people as in need of service or savior by their able bodied counterparts.

### Argument: What are the core argument(s) being made in the text?
- As a manifesto, Once the authors outline the primary intervention that they seek to take by defining The stakes of a Crip technoscience, They then moved to outline 4 core tenants of this Crip technoscience framework: 
    - (1) That disabled people are positioned not just as authorities on their own lived experiences , but they are recognized for their autonomy and active participant as world builders and designers whose design and maker practices are informed by that lived experience.
    - (2) A crip technoscience does not concern itself with singular or reductive view of accessibility, instead understanding that accessibility emerges not just from top down implementation of practices that enable the inclusion of disabled people into everyday space, but that accessibility itself emerges from political friction in which disabled people specifically forge their own pathways to ensure recognition and visibility.
    - (3) Crip technoscience embraces interdependence over independence, recognizing that Disability justice activists and advocates have relied on communal and intra community efforts to further their political goals . Furthermore, interdependence here also signifies a more complex understanding of the material and technological relations in which disabled people come into contact with, whether they be the materials that underlie the built environments that they navigate, the technologies and materials used in Mobility devices and other medical technologies, or even the institutional policies and norms that are imposed on disabled and able people alike.
    - (4) Crip Technoscience is explicitly focused on disability justice, rejecting discourses of compulsory able bodiedness that position disability as a deficit as opposed to a difference in embodied, material, and technological experience. likewise, such disability justice efforts are recognized for their interrelation with other Axes of oppression and marginalization, including pertaining to issues of sex, gender, sexuality, ethnicity, race, religion , and immigration status.

### Evidence: What evidence is used to support the argument(s)?
- There are several instances in which the authors draw on the history of disability justice activism in the United States and globally as evidence of the historical tendency of disabled people to powerfully, communally, and autonomously act to enact policies, develop technologies to meet their own needs, and reject the imposition of able bodied normative ideologies upon them.
