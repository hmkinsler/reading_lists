## Intervention, Argument, Evidence
### Intervention: What is the intervention that the scholar is making, and where is that intervention being made? In other words, how do you place that intervention relative to existing disciplinary conversations that are taking place?
The primary goal of this chapter is to develop Shivers-McNair’s relational rhetorics framework. To do so, She seeks to contribute to scholarship in rhetoric that moves Beyond written or oral forms of communication to instead consider the relations of materials, people, technology, animals, environments, and so on, as part of the relational milieu in which rhetorical work is occurring. 

### Argument: What are the core argument(s) being made in the text?
she ties together scholarship in science and technology studies, anthropology, new materialism, and rhetoric to make the argument that a “more-than-human” rhetorics enables rhetors to better understand “not only the making of technologies and objects, but also the making and remaking of bodies and relations—and all the possibilities and pitfalls entailed” (p. 33)
