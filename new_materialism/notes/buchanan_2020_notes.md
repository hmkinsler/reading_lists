## SQR3
### Survey: Skim the text and identify main ideas
- This chapter serves as the introduction for this book that focuses on assemblage Theory and how assembly just can be taken up as a method, with Buchanan appropriately citing John Law’s interest in the Mess of social sciences research. He explains that Law is not the only scholar that has taken up this concept, however, noting that De Landa has made use of the term himself and additionally argued that Deleuze and Guattari themselves employ it in nearly a dozen ways. His concern, then, is to respond to the seeming ability to refer to any collection or assortment or arrangement of things as an assemblage, thus offering some clarity and theoretical analytical refinement to this concept, especially given its stakes in research that seeks to understand the interconnectedness of any number of Agents or actors involved in complex Networks or systems.

To make things all the more complex, he explains that the notion of assembly has been taken up not only by new materialists, but also Scholars focused on actor Network theory, and even in a separate strain of scholarship that employees assembled in a completely different way and does not draw on the originating work from Deleuze and Guattari.
