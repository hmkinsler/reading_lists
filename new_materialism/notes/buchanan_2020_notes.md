## Intervention, Argument, Evidence
### Intervention: What is the intervention that the scholar is making, and where is that intervention being made? In other words, how do you place that intervention relative to existing disciplinary conversations that are taking place?
The major concern that Buchanan expresses in this chapter is that assemblage has been taken up in a variety of ways in the social sciences and humanities, and though that work is often built upon the concept of assemblage that comes from Deleuze and Guattari, He feels that the concept has been transformed in so many ways and applied so ubiquitously that its original ontological concerns have been lost in the process . For this reason, He tries to retrace Deleuze and Guattari's intent in introducing the concept and clarify its meaning.

### Argument: What are the core argument(s) being made in the text?
There are two major aspects of the concept of assemblage in Deleuze and Guattari's writing Buchanan feels has been lost in this recent strain of scholarship , most especially the role of desire And the tendency to treat the assemblage as a mechanistic and isolated apparatus, as opposed to one aspect of Deleuze and Guattari's metaphysics.

Based on interviews and additional writings from Deleuze, Buchanan explains that the concept of assemblage is in many ways meant to an alternative framing for behavior, "the assemblage is intended to answer several types of question, 'how?', 'why?', 'when?', and not just a 'what?' question" (p. 13). Here, we see a clear link between the metaphysical or ontological work that _ A thousand plateaus_ is attempting to perform and ethology, Or the study of the behavior of non human animals. What Buchanan offers a warning , however, is that ethology should not be understood as the work that the concept of assemblage is performing. Instead, The point is that D+G are not attempting to replace sociological or psychoanalytic modelings of human behavior with that of ethological models-- They are drawing on ethological models as the inspiration for their concepts in order to point out the non deterministic nature of behavior overall. For Buchanan, The major concern here is that scholars are unintentionally or incidentally reimposing deterministic or structural viewpoints onto the concept of assemblage in ways that defeat the purpose of its original conception by D+G.

### Evidence: What evidence is used to support the argument(s)?
Critique of other scholars' use of the concept of assemblage, including De Landa, Especially in conversation with D+G's extended discussion and reflections on the originating principles of the term beyond its introduction _ A thousand plateaus._
