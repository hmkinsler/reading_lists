##### Introduction: Moving Borders
The introduction begins with laying out nails project for this book, Which is to offer away of theorizing the border, of multiple kinds, whether they are informational, geopolitical, jurisdictional , economic, and so on. For Nail, however, " While the technologies of division themselves may differ throughout history according to who wields them, when, where, and so on, The cut or process of social division itself is what is common to all of its relative manifestations" (p. 2). He introduces the process by which these divisions take place, through breaks or bifurcation, which he argues can lead to both extensive and intensive divisions. He offers an introductory review of the characteristics of borders, which he argues are always in motion, are characterized by an ongoing process of circulation , and cannot be understood solely through the model of space. He explains that a theory of the border is important for understanding how social divisions have been produced and reproduced continuously throughout history, for which " This book presents a select history of four major types, their material conditions, and forces of social motion: the territorial, political, juridical, and economic forms of social division" (p. 16).

#### Part I: Theory of the Border
##### Border Kinopower
In this chapter, nail connects his understanding of borders as the force through which social division is implemented to kinopolitics, "the theory and analysis of social motion: the politics of movement" (p. 24). Here, he looks at the circulation of various kinds of flows as part of the means by which forces of motion produce various kinds of borders.

#### Part II: Historical Limology
In this part of the book, Nail is tracing the social motion and forces, or kinopolitics, of various kinds of borders, and the historical and social conditions that lead to their imposition, as well as the social transformations they enact.
