#### Rhizome
I have some notes that I took while reading this with the _Unruly Assemblages_ reading group during the fall 2026 semester:

Questions / Weaker Points:
- How are D+G taking up, extending, or otherwise responding to Bergson’s work? This seems especially notable for Deleuze. Related concepts especially needing clarification:
    - Temporality
    - Intensities
    - Virtual / real

- If this is the more methodological, and thus actively and stylistically experimental, of the Capitalism and Schizophrenia work, then how do we understand the application of this method in the context of academic scholarship? What does that look like?

- I feel like I need to refine my understanding of where the BwO or the plane of consistency / immanence meet with more stabilized formations (i.e. strata). In the first two chapters, the notion of the BwO makes more sense given the emphasis of these two plateaus on change, dynamics, circulations, and flows, whereas the role of the BwO in relation to stable forms in the third plateau is a bit less clear.

Things I Learned / Stronger Points:
- I have a stronger grasp of what distinguishes a rhizomatic approach as opposed to an arborescent one. I recently finished Suchman’s  (2007) Human-machine reconfigurations, and the resistance to binary or dualistic frameworks is reminiscent of her interrogation of the imposed cognitivist or behaviorist model of planning and action, the former of which results in the obscuring of the latter.

- I similarly resonated with their critique of Chomsky and structural approaches to linguistics.

- Plateaus as a way to understand following the line or trajectory of intensities.

- Pedagogical implications remind me of some of the critical responses to Shipka’s (2011) Toward a composition made whole.
